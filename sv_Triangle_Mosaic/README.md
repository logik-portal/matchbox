# sv_Triangle_Mosaic

## Description

Triangle mosaic with per-tile size, rotation and offset, edges, bevel and colour variation, and a tile-by-tile transition from Front to Back driven by a matte or a built-in wipe: tiles shrink, fade, spin, or flip over on a hinge or in place. Contact: sinan@sinanvural.com

## Flame Requirements

2017.0.0

## Supported Modes

- ❌ **Action**: Not supported
- ✅ **Transition**: Supported
- ❌ **Timeline**: Not supported

Matchbox
